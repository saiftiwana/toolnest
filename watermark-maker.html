Common Use Cases

Freelance photographers watermark portfolio previews before sharing proofs with a client. Tips for Best Results

Choose a text color that contrasts clearly with the busiest part of your photo — white text works well on dark backgrounds, while black text stands out on bright ones. Keep your watermark text short; long business names or URLs can become hard to read once scaled down for social media. If the default corner placement covers an important part of the image, simply crop or reframe the source photo before uploading it so the watermark lands in open space.
