How It Works

This photo watermark maker stamps your copyright text directly onto the canvas pixel data client-side — a free, unlimited alternative to Watermarkly with no daily limits, since nothing is ever sent to a server.

Are my copyrighted photos uploaded to a server when watermarking?

No — the watermark is drawn onto the image using your browser's canvas, and the original file never leaves your device.

How do I transparently watermark images for social media?

Choose a text color that contrasts with your photo and position it in a corner so it stays visible without covering the main subject.
