Tips for Best Results

Since YouTube crops banner images differently across devices, keep your most important text and graphics centered rather than near the edges, which is exactly what this tool's centered text placement is designed to support. Choose a background photo with a relatively simple, uncluttered area in the middle where your text will be overlaid for best readability. Preview how the banner looks cropped tighter, since mobile devices show a narrower center strip than the full desktop view.
