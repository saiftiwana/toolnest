How to Use the Property Loan EMI & Mortgage Calculator

  1. Pick a Mode: Choose Loan EMI for a straightforward loan, or Mortgage for a home purchase with a down payment.
  2. Enter Your Numbers: Loan EMI needs the loan amount, rate, and term; Mortgage additionally needs the property price and down payment.
  3. Calculate: Tap Calculate to see your monthly payment, total interest, and total payable instantly.
  4. Export: Copy the full breakdown or reset to try different numbers.

Why Use This Calculator

A free property loan EMI and mortgage calculator online means not switching between two separate tools to answer two closely related questions: "what's my monthly payment on this loan?" and "what's my monthly payment on this home, after my down payment?" This tool combines both into one connected calculator, so a borrower checking a personal or business loan and a home buyer planning a purchase both find what they need in the same place.

How It Works

Both modes use the standard EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) formula — a fixed monthly payment calculated from the principal, the monthly interest rate, and the total number of payments. Loan EMI applies this directly to the full loan amount. Mortgage mode first subtracts your down payment from the property price to find the actual loan amount, then applies the same formula, and also shows your down payment as a percentage of the property price.

Key Features

As a free calculator with no signup and zero server uploads, every calculation happens instantly and privately in your browser. Both modes show a full breakdown: monthly payment, total interest paid over the full term, and total amount payable — not just the headline monthly figure. This gives a realistic picture of the true cost of a loan before committing to a lender's offer.

Common Use Cases

Someone taking a personal or business loan uses Loan EMI to instantly see their monthly installment and total interest cost before signing anything. A home buyer comparing properties uses Mortgage mode to see how a larger down payment reduces their monthly payment, or to check whether a property fits their budget once real financing terms are applied — before falling in love with a home outside a comfortable price range.

Tips

A larger down payment directly reduces both your loan amount and your total interest paid over the life of the loan — even a modest increase in down payment can meaningfully lower your monthly payment. Always compare the total payable figure, not just the monthly payment, since a longer term can look more affordable monthly while costing significantly more in total interest.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this calculator really free to use?

Yes. Both the Loan EMI and Mortgage calculators are completely free with no signup, no limits, and no hidden charges.

Is my data uploaded or stored anywhere?

No. Every calculation runs locally in your browser using client-side JavaScript — nothing is ever sent to or stored on a server.

What's the difference between the two modes?

Loan EMI calculates a payment on the full loan amount you enter. Mortgage mode additionally accounts for a down payment, subtracting it from the property price before calculating your loan and monthly payment.

How is the monthly payment calculated?

Both modes use the standard EMI formula based on principal, monthly interest rate, and number of payments — the same formula used by banks and mortgage lenders.

Does this show total interest paid?

Yes, both calculators show total interest paid over the full loan term alongside the monthly payment and total amount payable.

Can I copy or save my result?

Yes, use the Copy Result button on either calculator to copy the full breakdown to your clipboard.
