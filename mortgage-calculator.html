Monthly mortgage payment including down payment — free, instant.

How to Use the Mortgage Calculator

  1. Enter Home Price & Down Payment: Input purchase price and down payment amount.
  2. Set Interest & Term: Input annual mortgage rate and loan period in years.
  3. Calculate Amortization: View monthly payment and total loan cost.

Why Use This Calculator

Calculating a fixed-rate home mortgage's monthly payment, factoring in a down payment, online for free helps home buyers budget accurately before house hunting. This tool computes exact monthly payment and total interest cost based on home price, down payment, rate, and term.

How It Works

This mortgage-payment-estimator subtracts your down payment from the total home price to determine the loan amount, then applies the standard mortgage payment formula based on monthly interest rate and total number of payments. It's a free, 100% private browser alternative to Zillow and Bankrate mortgage calculators for home purchase planning.

Key Features

The fixed-rate-mortgage-calculator clearly separates loan amount (after down payment) from the original home price, then computes monthly payment and total cost over the full loan term. As a down-payment-mortgage tool, adjusting the down payment amount instantly shows how it changes the resulting monthly payment. The total interest figure over the full loan life often comes as a surprise, making it a useful number to see clearly before committing. The down payment now also displays as a percentage of the property price, and a Copy Result button captures the complete summary for comparison across different lenders.

Common Use Cases

Home buyers calculate expected monthly mortgage payments before beginning a house search with a realistic budget. Buyers compare how different down payment amounts affect their monthly obligation. Financial advisors help clients understand mortgage affordability across different term lengths, and buyers comparing fixed-rate offers from different lenders check calculations independently. Real estate agents run quick mortgage scenarios with clients during an initial consultation meeting.

Tips for Best Results

Try adjusting the down payment percentage to see its meaningful impact on monthly payment — even a modest increase in down payment can noticeably reduce the loan amount and resulting monthly obligation. Remember this calculator covers principal and interest only; actual monthly housing costs often also include property tax, insurance, and possibly private mortgage insurance not captured here. Compare the total interest figure across a few different term lengths side by side to fully appreciate the long-term cost tradeoff.

A Quick Note

Understanding the full monthly commitment, not just the sticker price of a home, leads to more confident and financially sound home-buying decisions.

Privacy & Security

Because ToolNest processes everything entirely within your browser using client-side JavaScript, your files never leave your device. There is no server-side storage, no third-party analytics attached to your data, and no risk of a breach exposing files you processed months ago.

How does down payment amount affect monthly mortgage payments?

A larger down payment reduces the loan amount needed, directly lowering the resulting monthly payment and total interest paid over the loan term.

What is an amortization schedule in mortgage repayment?

It's a schedule showing how each payment splits between principal and interest over the loan's life, with interest portion decreasing over time.

Does this calculator include property tax and insurance in the payment?

No, it calculates principal and interest only; actual monthly costs often include additional taxes and insurance not shown here.

Can I compare different down payment scenarios using this tool?

Yes, simply change the down payment field and recalculate to instantly compare how it affects your monthly payment.
