Tips for Best Results

Remember that CMYK-to-RGB conversion is an approximation, since the two color spaces represent color differently (subtractive ink mixing versus additive light) — always confirm critical brand colors with an actual printed proof rather than relying solely on a calculated conversion. For the most accurate print results, work with your print shop's specific color profile when precision matters.

What is the difference between CMYK for print and HEX/RGB for web screens?

CMYK represents color through subtractive ink mixing used in printing, while HEX/RGB represents color through additive light mixing used on screens.

How to quickly convert print design CMYK codes into web CSS colors?

Is the CMYK to RGB conversion always exactly accurate?

It's a close mathematical approximation; exact print color matching may require your printer's specific color profile.
