How can I find my public IP address and ISP information instantly?

Simply open the tool and your public IP, ISP, and approximate location are detected and displayed automatically.

Is my location exact when checked via an IP lookup tool?

No, the location shown is approximate, typically accurate to the city or region level based on ISP registration data, not your precise address.

Why does my IP address sometimes change between visits?

Many home internet connections use dynamic IP addresses that can change periodically, which is normal and controlled by your ISP.

Can I use this tool to verify my VPN is working?

Yes, checking the reported location before and after connecting to a VPN can help confirm whether your traffic is being routed correctly.
