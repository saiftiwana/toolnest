Why Use This Tool

Building a professional resume online for free, with no watermark and no account required, is exactly what most job seekers need for a quick, polished CV. This tool offers three distinct template styles and exports directly to a clean PDF, ready to attach to a job application. It cuts out the frustrating paywall many resume sites hide behind after you have already filled in your details.

How It Works

This CV maker and resume PDF exporter takes your entered details — name, summary, experience, education, and skills — and renders them into a structured layout using your chosen template's colors, fonts, and section styling, generating a real PDF client-side using a JavaScript PDF library. It's a free, no-signup alternative to Canva Resume and Zety for anyone who needs a professional resume without a subscription.

Key Features

The professional-resume-generator now offers six distinct visual styles: Classic, Modern, Minimal Serif, Creative (with an accent side bar), Executive (centered, formal), and Compact (tighter spacing for longer resumes) — each customizable with your own accent color and choice of font. As a job-application-document-tool, the live preview updates as you fill in fields, and the exported PDF matches the template styling with proper section headers, spacing, and page breaks for longer content. Experience, education, and skills are added and removed as individual entries rather than one big text block, and five additional optional sections — Certifications, Languages, Projects, Awards, and References — can each be added only when relevant to your background. A photo upload adds a profile picture to the header, a Download ATS-Friendly Text option exports a plain-text version formatted for applicant tracking systems that struggle with styled PDFs, and Save Draft / Load Draft keeps your in-progress resume in this browser between visits.

Common Use Cases

Job seekers build a first resume without hiring a designer or purchasing resume software. Students creating their first CV for an internship application use a template that looks professional despite limited work history. Career changers update their resume with a fresh template style when applying to a new industry, and freelancers maintain multiple resume versions tailored to different types of client work. Recent graduates build their very first professional resume for an entry-level job search.

Tips for Best Results

Keep your professional summary concise — two to three sentences that highlight your strongest qualifications tend to work better than a lengthy paragraph. List experience and education entries with the most recent first, since that's the standard convention most hiring managers expect.

A Quick Note

A clean, well-organized resume format helps a hiring manager quickly find the details that matter most, which can make a real difference during the first quick scan of an application.

What information is essential for a professional CV or resume?

A strong resume typically includes contact information, a summary, work experience, education, and relevant skills, all of which this tool organizes automatically.

Which resume template style works best for different industries?

Modern and Creative suit design and tech roles, Classic and Executive work broadly across corporate industries, Minimal Serif fits traditional or academic contexts, and Compact helps fit a longer career history onto fewer pages.

Does the exported PDF include a watermark?

No, the downloaded resume PDF is a clean export with no watermark or branding added.

Can I switch templates after entering my information?

Yes, your entered details stay in the form fields, so you can preview different templates, accent colors, and fonts without retyping anything.

What is an ATS-friendly resume and why does it matter?

Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) scan resumes for keywords and structure before a human ever sees them, and heavily styled PDFs can sometimes be parsed incorrectly — the Download ATS-Friendly Text option exports a clean plain-text version formatted for reliable parsing.

Can I add sections like Certifications, Languages, or Projects?

Yes, five optional sections — Certifications, Languages, Projects, Awards, and References — can each be added independently and only appear on your resume if you fill them in.

Can I save my progress and come back later?

Yes, click Save Draft to store your current resume in this browser, then Load Draft on a future visit to continue where you left off.

Can I add a photo to my resume?

Yes, uploading a photo is optional and adds a circular profile picture to the resume header — note that photos are not recommended for resumes in some countries and industries, so check local norms first.
