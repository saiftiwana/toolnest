Everything a New Channel Needs Before Uploading

A video that nobody clicks is indistinguishable from a video that does not exist, and for most small channels the reason is not the footage but the packaging around it. These four tools cover that packaging. The YouTube Branding Kit produces correctly sized thumbnails, channel banners and profile images, working from the exact pixel dimensions and safe areas YouTube expects, so your banner is not cropped awkwardly on a phone or a television. The Title Generator builds title options around your topic and keyword, with a live character count so you know what will be visible before the text is cut off in search results. The Description Template Builder assembles a reusable structure with your summary, timestamps, links and social handles already laid out. The Tag & Keyword Idea Generator produces related search phrases to consider. Everything is free, produces no watermark, and requires no account or channel connection.

How to Use These Tools in Order

Work backwards from the search result. Start with the Tag & Keyword Idea Generator to see the phrases people actually type around your subject, then feed the strongest of those into the Title Generator so your title contains a phrase somebody might search for rather than only a phrase that sounds clever. Keep the visible part of the title under roughly sixty characters, because that is where mobile search results typically truncate. Next, design the thumbnail in the Branding Kit: use few words, make them large enough to read at the size of a postage stamp, and make sure the image still reads clearly when the duration badge covers the bottom right corner. Finally, build your description template once and reuse it for every upload, putting the important sentence and any link in the first two lines, since that is all viewers see before pressing show more.

No Watermark, No Channel Access, No Sign Up

Most free thumbnail and banner makers either stamp a logo across your work or require you to create an account and, increasingly, to connect your actual YouTube channel through OAuth. Connecting a channel grants a third party real permissions over your account, which is a heavy price for a picture. ToolNest asks for none of that. The Branding Kit draws your thumbnail on an HTML5 canvas inside your browser and hands you the finished PNG as a download, with nothing overlaid on it. The text tools generate their suggestions locally from the words you type. No channel is linked, no analytics are read, no email address is collected, and the images you create are yours to use commercially without attribution.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size should a YouTube thumbnail be?

1280 by 720 pixels, a 16:9 ratio, under 2MB, in JPG or PNG. The Branding Kit exports at exactly this size, so it will always be accepted.

What are the correct channel banner dimensions?

Upload at 2560 by 1440 pixels, but keep all important text and logos inside the central 1546 by 423 safe area, because that is the only region visible on every device.

Do the generated thumbnails have a watermark?

No. Everything exported from the Branding Kit is clean, with no ToolNest logo or branding, and free to use on monetised channels.

Do tags still affect YouTube search rankings?

Their influence is modest today compared with the title, thumbnail and description, and they mainly help YouTube understand misspellings and context. Treat generated tags as a supporting signal, not a strategy.

Do I need to connect my YouTube channel?

No. These tools never request access to your account. Nothing is linked, no permissions are granted, and no sign-up is required.
