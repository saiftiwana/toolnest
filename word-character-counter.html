Why Use This Tool

A real-time word and character counter with reading time built in is essential whenever you need to count words, sentences, and paragraphs online without switching between a document editor and a separate counting tool. How It Works

This letter counter and sentence analyzer recalculates word count, character count, and estimated reading time on every keystroke using simple client-side string parsing — no page reload or server round-trip required. It's a free, lightweight browser alternative to WordCounter.net and CharacterCountOnline for anyone who wants instant feedback while drafting.

Key Features

The paragraph counter and reading time estimator update simultaneously as you type, so you always know exactly how long your document has become. Because the tool is a character limit checker as well, it's especially useful for platforms like Twitter/X, meta descriptions, or SMS messages that enforce strict character caps. There is no minimum or maximum text length restriction, so the counter works equally well for a short tweet draft or a full multi-page document. Beyond words and reading time, it now also breaks down character count with and without spaces, plus sentence, line, and paragraph counts, all updating live as you type.

Tips for Best Results

Remember that character counts with and without spaces can differ significantly for longer documents — always check which limit your target platform actually enforces. Reading time estimates are based on an average reading speed, so actual time will vary depending on the complexity of your content.

Privacy & Security

Because ToolNest processes everything entirely within your browser using client-side JavaScript, your files never leave your device. There is no server-side storage, no third-party analytics attached to your data, and no risk of a breach exposing files you processed months ago.

How does word count calculation work with spaces and special characters?

Words are counted by splitting text on whitespace, while character counts can include or exclude spaces depending on which stat you check.

Can I check my document length without pasting text onto external servers?

Yes — this counter runs entirely in your browser, so your text is never sent anywhere.

Does the tool count punctuation as part of the word count?

Punctuation attached to a word (like a period or comma) doesn't create an extra word, but it is included in the character count.

How accurate is the reading time estimate?

It's based on an average adult reading speed of about 200 words per minute, so actual reading time may vary by reader.
