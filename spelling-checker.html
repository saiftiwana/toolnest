How to Use the Spelling Checker

  1. Paste Document: Insert your draft, email, or content block into the proofreading field.
  2. Run Scan: Your browser's built-in spellcheck automatically underlines typos as you type.
  3. Apply Corrections: Right-click any flagged word to view suggested corrections and update your text.

Why Use This Tool

An instant online spelling checker that's private and client-side is useful whenever you want a quick typo scan without pasting your draft into a third-party grammar service. This tool checks English text for spelling typos fast, using your own browser's built-in proofreading engine. It provides a fast, zero-friction way to catch obvious mistakes before sending a message or publishing a comment publicly.

How It Works

This spell-check tool and text-proofreader activates your browser's native spellcheck attribute on an editable text field, underlining misspelled words exactly the way your browser already flags typos in any text box. It's a free, instant, local alternative to LanguageTool and Grammarly's spell check for a lightweight typo scan without an account or a heavier grammar-checking service.

Key Features

Because the fix-spelling-typos and correct-misspelled-words process relies on your browser's own dictionary, it works entirely offline once the page has loaded, with zero data sent anywhere for checking. As a writing checker, it's ideal for quick scans of shorter text like an email, comment, or social media post before publishing. Because it uses the exact same spellcheck engine already built into your browser, there is nothing new to learn — the experience feels identical to typing in any other text field on your device.

Common Use Cases

Anyone drafting a quick email wants a final typo check before hitting send. Students proofread a short paragraph or comment before submitting it online. Non-native English speakers double-check spelling on informal writing like social media captions, and writers catch obvious typos in a first draft before a more thorough editing pass. Customer support agents proofread canned responses before sending them to a client to maintain a professional tone.

Tips for Best Results

Right-click (or long-press on mobile) any underlined word to see your browser's built-in spelling suggestions and apply a correction directly. For a more thorough grammar and style review beyond basic spelling, pair this quick check with a full read-through of your text afterward. If your browser is set to a different language than your text, switch the browser language setting first so the correct dictionary is used for underlining. A visible placeholder now guides you before typing, the box has more breathing room for comfortable writing, and a Clear button resets it instantly.

Privacy & Security

Because ToolNest processes everything entirely within your browser using client-side JavaScript, your files never leave your device. There is no server-side storage, no third-party analytics attached to your data, and no risk of a breach exposing files you processed months ago.
