Check how strong a password is before you use it — free, 100% private.

    How to Use the Password Strength Tester

    1. Type Password: Enter the password you wish to evaluate into the input field.
    2. Check Score: View a live strength meter analyzing length, characters, and symbols.
    3. Review Rating: See a clear rating from Very Weak to Very Strong before finalizing your password.

    Why Use This Tool

    Checking password strength before creating an account online, entirely client side, avoids the risk of typing a real password into a form that transmits it somewhere for analysis. This tool checks length, character variety, and symbol usage instantly, with your password never leaving your browser. It offers peace of mind precisely because nothing typed here is ever logged, cached, or sent anywhere for analysis.

    How It Works

    This password security checker and strength meter scores your input based on five criteria — length over 8 characters, length over 12 characters, mixed case letters, numbers, and special characters — updating the visual strength bar and rating label live as you type. It's a free, private alternative to online password checkers that require submitting your actual password to a remote server.

    Key Features

    The strong-password-validator gives instant visual feedback through a colored strength bar plus a plain-language rating from Very Weak to Very Strong, making it easy to understand at a glance whether a password needs improvement. Because the entire scoring happens client-side, there's zero risk of your typed password being logged, stored, or transmitted anywhere. The color-coded strength bar updates on every keystroke, giving immediate visual feedback without needing to click a separate check button. A detailed checklist now shows exactly which criteria your password meets — length, uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols — along with plain-language suggestions for what to add if it falls short, and a Copy Password button for convenience since everything stays on your own device.

    Common Use Cases

    Anyone creating a new account tests a candidate password's strength before submitting a signup form. IT administrators demonstrate to employees what makes a password weak versus strong during a security training session. Developers building their own signup flow reference this tool's scoring logic as inspiration for their own validation rules, and security-conscious users routinely check an existing password's strength rating. Small business owners setting up shared team accounts test candidate passwords before distributing them to staff.

    Tips for Best Results

    Aim for at least 12 characters combined with a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols to reach a Strong or Very Strong rating. Avoid common patterns like sequential numbers or keyboard rows (like 'qwerty'), since these are easily guessed even if they technically meet length and character-variety requirements.

    Privacy & Security

    Because ToolNest processes everything entirely within your browser using client-side JavaScript, your files never leave your device. There is no server-side storage, no third-party analytics attached to your data, and no risk of a breach exposing files you processed months ago.

    Is my password ever sent to a server when checking its strength?

    No, the entire strength calculation happens locally in your browser using JavaScript — your password is never transmitted or stored anywhere.

    What factors determine a strong password score?

    Length, use of uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters all contribute to the strength score.

    How long should a strong password be?

    Security best practices generally recommend at least 12 characters combined with varied character types for strong protection.

    Does a high strength score guarantee a password is safe?

    A high score indicates good complexity, but avoiding reused or predictable passwords matters just as much as raw character variety.
