Frequently Asked Questions

Can I manage all three academic metrics (GPA, CGPA, attendance) in one toolkit?

Yes, the GPA Calculator, CGPA & Multi-Semester, and Attendance Planner tabs share one dashboard that updates as you use each one.

Is my student academic data completely private and device-local?

Yes, all calculations happen in your browser, and Save to This Browser stores data using your browser's local storage only — nothing is sent to a server.

How do different institutions' grading scales affect my calculations?

The GPA Calculator uses a standard 4.0-scale letter grade mapping, and CGPA-to-percentage conversion multipliers are adjustable, but always confirm your specific institution's exact scale for official purposes.

Can I calculate GPA for multiple semesters cumulatively?

Yes, the Multi-Semester tracker lets you log each semester's GPA and credit hours to calculate an overall credit-weighted cumulative CGPA.

How accurate is the CGPA to percentage conversion across universities?

It's a general estimate using common multipliers (like 9.5 for a 10-point scale); exact formulas vary by institution.

What attendance percentage is typically required to remain eligible?

Requirements vary by institution, commonly falling between 75% and 85% — set your own target percentage in the Attendance Planner to match your school's policy.

Can I project how my attendance will change if I attend upcoming classes?

Yes, the Scenario Planner calculates your projected percentage if you attend a set number of upcoming classes, in addition to the classes needed or missable for your target.

Does the toolkit work offline without internet connection?

The calculators work fully offline once the page is loaded; only the optional PDF export needs an internet connection the first time to load its library.
