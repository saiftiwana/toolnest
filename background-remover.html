Common Use Cases

E-commerce sellers cut product photos free of cluttered backgrounds before uploading to a store or marketplace. Graphic designers isolate a subject quickly to composite it into a new scene. Tips for Best Results

Photos with clear contrast between the subject and background produce the cleanest edges — busy or low-contrast backgrounds may need a second pass. Good, even lighting on the subject also helps the AI model draw a more accurate boundary around hair and fine details. If the AI leaves a faint edge fringe around fine hair or fur, try a photo with a more evenly lit, higher-contrast background for a cleaner cutout next time.
