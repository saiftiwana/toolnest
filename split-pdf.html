How to Split a PDF Online

  1. Import PDF: Upload the PDF document you want to divide or separate.
  2. Select Pages: Enter specific page numbers or page ranges (e.g., 1, 3-5, 8) to extract.
  3. Download Pages: Click "Extract Pages" to create and save your new standalone PDF file(s) as a ZIP.

How It Works

This PDF page extractor and page splitter separates the document's pages directly in your browser using local PDF parsing — a free, secure client-side alternative to iLovePDF's split PDF tool for anyone who wants to pull specific pages out of a PDF privately.

How can I delete or separate pages from a PDF document for free?

Enter the page numbers or ranges you want to extract, and the tool will generate a new PDF containing just those pages.

Is PDF page splitting completely private in the browser?

Yes — splitting happens entirely client-side, so your original PDF is never uploaded anywhere.
