How to Use the Split PDF Tool

  1. Import PDF: Upload the PDF document you want to divide or separate.
  2. Select Pages: Enter specific page numbers or page ranges (e.g., 1, 3-5, 8) to extract.
  3. Download Pages: Click "Extract Pages" to create and save your new standalone PDF file(s) as a ZIP.

Why Use This Tool

Needing to extract pages from a PDF online for free, with no sign up, comes up whenever you only need a section of a longer document — a single chapter, a signed page, or a specific form. This tool lets you split a PDF document into individual pages or a custom range entirely privately, right in your browser. It saves the trouble of printing a full document just to physically remove pages you do not need before re-scanning the rest.

How It Works

This PDF page extractor and page splitter separates the document's pages directly in your browser using local PDF parsing, then packages the requested pages into a downloadable ZIP archive. It's a free, secure client-side alternative to iLovePDF's split PDF tool for anyone who wants to pull specific pages out of a PDF privately, without a file ever touching an external server.

Key Features

The tool accepts flexible page range syntax — single numbers, ranges like 3-5, or a mix such as 1, 3-5, 8 — so you can extract exactly the pages you need in one operation. Leaving the range field blank simply splits every page into its own separate file, useful when you need each page of a document as an individual PDF. Even a document with a hundred pages splits quickly, since the browser only needs to copy the specific pages you requested rather than reprocess the entire file.

Common Use Cases

Lawyers extract a single signed page from a lengthy contract to send as proof of agreement. Students pull out just the relevant chapter from a large textbook PDF for focused studying. Accountants separate individual invoice pages from a combined scanned batch, and HR staff extract a single employee's record from a larger multi-page report. Publishers extract a single article or chapter from a larger compiled PDF to share independently.

Tips for Best Results

Double-check your page numbers against the total page count shown after uploading, since requesting a page beyond the document's length is simply ignored rather than producing an error. For extracting many individual pages at once, leaving the range field blank and letting the tool split every page is often faster than typing out a long list.

Privacy & Security

Because ToolNest processes everything entirely within your browser using client-side JavaScript, your files never leave your device. There is no server-side storage, no third-party analytics attached to your data, and no risk of a breach exposing files you processed months ago.
