How to Use the Final Grade Calculator

  1. Input Current Scores: Enter your assignment, midterm, and final percentages.
  2. Set Weights: Enter each component's weight percentage.
  3. Calculate: View your final weighted grade instantly.

Why Use This Calculator

Calculating a final course grade from weighted assignments, midterm, and final exam scores online for free removes the guesswork of combining differently-weighted components manually. This tool applies each component's exact weight percentage to produce an accurate overall grade instantly.

How It Works

This weighted-grade-calculator multiplies each score by its corresponding weight percentage, sums these weighted contributions, then divides by the total weight to produce the final grade — handling cases where weights don't sum to exactly 100% by normalizing proportionally. It's a free, instant, local alternative to RogerHub's Final Grade Calculator for course grade planning.

Key Features

The course-grade-predictor accepts any combination of weighted components — assignments, midterm, and final exam — with fully customizable weight percentages for each. As an exam-weight-calculator, it flags if your entered weights don't sum to exactly 100%, helping catch a data entry mistake before relying on the result. The full breakdown now shows exactly how many points each component contributed toward the final grade, and an optional target-grade field calculates the exact score needed on the final exam to reach a specific overall goal.

Common Use Cases

Students calculate their current course standing before a final exam to understand what score they need. Students planning study time prioritize based on which component carries the most weight toward their final grade. Teaching assistants verify grade calculations match a syllabus's stated weighting scheme, and students petitioning a grade dispute recalculate using the syllabus's official weights to confirm accuracy. Students on academic probation carefully track their projected final grade every week to stay on top of requirements.

Tips for Best Results

Double-check your syllabus for the exact weight percentages your instructor uses, since guessing or misremembering weights will produce an inaccurate final grade projection. If you don't yet have a final exam score, try entering different hypothetical scores to see what range of outcomes is possible for your final grade. Save a screenshot of the calculation for your own records in case a grade discrepancy needs to be discussed with an instructor later.

A Quick Note

Knowing exactly how much a final exam matters relative to earlier coursework helps set realistic study priorities during a busy exam period.
