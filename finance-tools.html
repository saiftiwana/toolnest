Nine Calculators for Everyday Money Decisions

Financial questions are usually arithmetic questions wearing a disguise, and these nine tools handle the arithmetic. The SIP & Investment Return Calculator projects what a regular monthly contribution grows to at an assumed rate of return over a chosen period, separating your own contributions from the gains so you can see how much of the result is compounding rather than saving. The Simple & Compound Interest Calculator does the same for lump sums and shows the difference the compounding frequency makes. The Sales Tax / GST Calculator adds or removes tax from a figure, which matters when quoting inclusive or exclusive prices. The Crypto / Stock Profit & Loss Calculator accounts for entry price, exit price, quantity and fees. The Inflation Calculator shows what a sum's purchasing power becomes over time. The Salary / Take-Home Pay Calculator, Savings Goal Calculator, Zakat & Ushr Studio and Crypto Trading Command Center complete the set.

Reading the Numbers Honestly

Every projection here depends entirely on the rate of return you type in, and that figure is an assumption rather than a fact. A calculator will happily compound fifteen percent annually for twenty years and produce an impressive total, but no investment guarantees that rate, and the same calculation at seven percent tells a very different story. Run each projection at a pessimistic, a moderate and an optimistic rate before making a decision, and pay attention to what the Inflation Calculator shows alongside it, because a nominal return that merely matches inflation has preserved value rather than created it. When comparing a savings product against a loan, remember that compounding frequency and fees often matter more than the headline percentage. These tools are calculators, not financial advice, and ToolNest is not a licensed adviser.

Nothing About Your Money Is Transmitted

Salary figures, savings balances, investment positions and Zakat calculations describe your financial life in detail, and that information has obvious value to anyone who collects it. None of these tools sends what you type to a server. The mathematics is deterministic JavaScript executing in your browser, so your income, your portfolio size and your net worth stay on your device. There is no account, no email capture, no profile built from your inputs and no data shared with any advertiser or financial institution. This also means the calculators work with your connection switched off. If a tool offers to save your figures for next time, it uses your browser's own local storage, and clearing your browser data removes them completely.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do these calculators give financial advice?

No. They perform arithmetic on the figures you supply. ToolNest is not a licensed financial adviser, and any decision involving significant money should be discussed with a qualified professional who knows your circumstances.

What return rate should I assume for a SIP projection?

There is no correct answer, because future returns are unknown. Run the projection at several rates, including a deliberately pessimistic one, and treat the spread of results as the realistic picture rather than the best case.

Is my salary or investment data stored?

No. Every calculation runs locally in your browser and nothing is transmitted. There is no account and no database holding your figures.

Does the take-home pay calculator match my country's tax rules?

It applies the deductions and rates you enter, so it is only as accurate as those inputs. Tax law varies by country and changes yearly, so confirm current rates before relying on the result.

How is Zakat calculated in the Zakat & Ushr Studio?

It applies the standard 2.5 percent rate to qualifying wealth held above the nisab threshold for a lunar year, with Ushr handled separately for agricultural produce. Scholars differ on some categories, so consult a knowledgeable authority for your specific situation.
