How to Use the Crypto / Stock Profit & Loss Calculator

  1. Enter Order Details: Input quantity, buy price, and sell price per unit.
  2. Include Trading Fees: Add any buy/sell transaction fees.
  3. Calculate ROI: View net profit/loss and percentage Return on Investment.

Why Use This Calculator

Calculating profit or loss on a crypto or stock trade, including trading fees, online for free helps traders quickly evaluate a completed or planned trade. This tool computes net gain or loss along with percentage ROI in seconds, accounting for buy and sell fees.

How It Works

This stock-profit-calculator multiplies quantity by buy price to determine total cost, then multiplies quantity by sell price and subtracts fees to determine net proceeds, calculating the difference as profit or loss and expressing it as a percentage ROI. It's a free, privacy-safe alternative to CoinTracker's profit calculator and Investopedia's trading tools for quick trade math.

Key Features

The crypto-gain-estimator factors in trading fees directly into the net proceeds calculation, giving a more realistic profit figure than a simple price-difference calculation that ignores transaction costs. As a net-profit-margin tool, results clearly label whether the outcome is a Profit or Loss, along with the exact percentage return relative to the initial investment. Buy and sell fees are now entered separately for a more accurate net figure, the full breakdown shows total buy value, total sell value, gross P/L, total fees, and net P/L together, and a Copy Result button captures everything at once.

Common Use Cases

Cryptocurrency traders quickly check their profit or loss after closing a position, including exchange fees. Stock investors calculate the percentage return on a completed trade for their own records. Day traders evaluate multiple hypothetical trade scenarios before committing capital, and anyone reviewing past trading history calculates the actual net return after accounting for fees. Portfolio trackers manually log completed trade outcomes into a personal spreadsheet using this calculator first.

Tips for Best Results

Always include both buy-side and sell-side fees in the total fees field for the most accurate net profit figure, since many exchanges and brokers charge fees on both ends of a trade. Remember that this calculation reflects a single trade in isolation and doesn't account for tax implications, which vary significantly by jurisdiction.

A Quick Note

Trading involves real financial risk, and past results calculated here reflect only the specific numbers entered, not a prediction of future performance.

How do trading fees impact overall crypto or stock net profit/loss calculations?

Trading fees reduce net proceeds from a sale, so including them gives a more accurate profit or loss figure than ignoring transaction costs.

How to calculate exact percentage ROI (Return on Investment) from buy and sell prices?

ROI is calculated as net profit divided by total cost, multiplied by 100 to express it as a percentage.

Does this calculator account for tax on trading profits?

No, this tool calculates raw trading profit or loss only; consult a tax professional regarding tax implications specific to your jurisdiction.

Can I use this calculator for both cryptocurrency and stock trades?

Yes, the calculation logic applies equally to any asset type where you have a buy price, sell price, and quantity.
