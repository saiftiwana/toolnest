Tips for Best Results

For the clearest scanned text, photograph documents in good, even lighting and hold the camera directly overhead to avoid a skewed perspective. Uploading images in the exact order you want them to appear saves time, since the converter preserves your original selection order. Avoid mixing portrait and landscape photos if a uniform look matters, since each page sizes to fit the image as uploaded.
