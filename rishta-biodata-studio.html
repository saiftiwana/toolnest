Rishta Biodata Guide

What is a Rishta Biodata?

A Rishta biodata (marriage biodata) is a short personal and family profile shared for marriage introductions, typically covering personal details, education, career, family background, and partner preferences.

What information should be included?

Commonly included: name, age, education, profession, city, and family basics. Exactly how much detail to include is a personal and family choice — this tool never forces any field.

How much family information should be shared?

This varies by family preference — some biodatas include detailed sibling information, others keep it brief. All family fields here are optional.

Should phone numbers be included?

Many families prefer to share contact details privately and directly rather than printing them on a widely-shared document — this is a personal choice.

How to choose a biodata photo?

A recent, clear, individual photo is commonly preferred over group photos or heavily filtered images.

How to make a professional biodata?

Keep information accurate and concise, use a clean template, and only include sections that are genuinely relevant to you.

What should be avoided?

Avoid unnecessary sensitive information you're not comfortable sharing widely, and avoid leaving placeholder text like "N/A" for fields you'd rather omit entirely.

How to share biodata safely on WhatsApp?

Generate your WhatsApp-ready text or export a PDF, then share it only with people and families you trust, using WhatsApp's normal sharing controls.

English vs Urdu biodata

An English biodata is often preferred for professional/international contexts, while an Urdu biodata may feel more traditional and familiar to some families — this tool supports both.

Bilingual biodata

A bilingual English-Urdu biodata presents both language versions clearly, useful when different family members have different language preferences.

How to prepare a family-friendly biodata

Involve family members in reviewing the details before sharing, since a biodata often represents the whole family, not just the individual.
