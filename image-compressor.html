How to Compress an Image Online

  1. Upload Image: Drag and drop or select your JPG, PNG, or WebP photo into the browser tool.
  2. Adjust Compression: Use the live quality slider to set your target file size reduction in KB.
  3. Download File: Click "Compress" to instantly save your optimized image without any server uploads.

How It Works

This image size reducer and photo optimizer runs the lossy compression math entirely inside your browser's canvas engine — no file ever leaves your device. It's a free, privacy-focused alternative to TinyPNG and iloveimg for anyone who wants to shrink a picture file fast without giving up control of their photos.

Is image compression private when processed inside the browser?

Yes. The compression happens entirely on your device using client-side JavaScript. Your photo is never uploaded to any server.

How can I compress images without losing quality for web design?

Use the quality slider to find a balance — staying above 70–80% quality typically keeps images visually sharp while still reducing file size significantly.
