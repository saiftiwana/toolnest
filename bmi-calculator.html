How is Body Mass Index (BMI) calculated using height and weight?

BMI is calculated as weight in kilograms divided by height in meters squared, giving a standardized score used for general population screening.

What are the standard weight categories defined by health standards?

The commonly used categories are Underweight (below 18.5), Normal weight (18.5-24.9), Overweight (25-29.9), and Obese (30 and above).

Does BMI account for muscle mass or body composition?

No, BMI is based only on height and weight, so it doesn't distinguish between muscle and fat mass.

Is BMI a reliable measure of health for everyone?

BMI works reasonably well as a general population screening tool but has known limitations for athletes, older adults, and certain body types.
