Free Crypto Trading Journal

A crypto trading journal is a structured record of every trade you take — entry, exit, size, risk, and the reasoning behind it — kept so patterns in your own behavior become visible over time. Serious traders keep one because memory is unreliable and emotions distort recall: a journal shows what actually happened, not what you remember happening. This tool is a completely free, local-first crypto trading journal — no account, no upload, and no subscription required for the full feature set.

Track Your Crypto Trades

After every trade, record the asset, direction, entry and exit price, position size, stop loss, take profit, fees, and the setup you used. The journal automatically calculates your gross and net P/L, P/L percentage, initial risk, R multiple, and trade duration — deterministic math, not AI guesswork — so your numbers are consistent and auditable.

Build Your Trading Setup Library

Define your own repeatable setups (for example, a breakout-retest with specific confirmation rules), attach each trade to a setup, and see win rate, average R, total R, and profit factor per setup. This turns "I feel like my breakout trades work well" into an actual, checkable number.

Review Trades & Track Mistakes

The Trade Review workflow prompts you before and after every trade: why you entered, whether you followed your plan, whether you moved your stop, exited early, or chased price. Mistakes are tagged (FOMO, revenge trading, overtrading, and more) and aggregated so your single biggest recurring leak becomes visible instead of buried across dozens of individual trades.

Analyze Crypto Trading Performance

Win rate, average win/loss, profit factor, expectancy, max drawdown, and streaks are calculated from your real trade history — never estimated. Expectancy in particular (win rate × average win, minus loss rate × average loss) tells you what you can expect to make per trade on average, which matters more long-term than any single win or loss.

Trading Calendar & Equity Curve

A monthly calendar colors each trading day green, red, or neutral so you can spot clusters of losing days at a glance, while the equity curve (cumulative R) shows your trajectory over time using lightweight SVG rendering — no heavy charting library required.

Analyze Coins, Sessions & Setups

Break performance down by asset, trading session (Asia, London, New York, overlap), long vs short, day of week, and holding time. This is where genuinely useful patterns tend to hide — for example, a trader might discover their London-session breakout trades outperform every other combination by a wide margin.

Build Your Trading DNA

Trading DNA combines your best setup, best asset, best session, best day, top recurring mistake, and average R into one profile — purely statistical analysis of your own historical data, never a prediction or trading signal.

Crypto Trading Journal FAQ

Is the crypto trading journal free?

Yes, the entire journal and analytics system is free with no account or subscription required.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up — your data is tied to this browser only.

Does the journal work offline?

Yes. Adding, editing, and reviewing trades, setups, and analytics all work without an internet connection.

Does the tool upload my trading data?

No. All data is stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB and is never sent to any server.

Can I save chart screenshots?

Yes, before-entry and after-exit screenshots can be attached to each trade and are stored locally in your browser.

Can I create custom trading setups?

Yes, the Setup Library lets you define your own setups with custom rules and track performance per setup.

Can I track trading mistakes?

Yes, the Mistake Tracker lets you tag recurring mistakes like FOMO, revenge trading, and overtrading, then see their frequency and R impact.

Can I analyze my crypto trading performance?

Yes, the Performance section covers win rate, profit factor, expectancy, drawdown, equity curve, and breakdowns by coin, session, direction, day, and holding time.

Can I export my trading data?

Yes, you can export a full JSON backup or a CSV of your trades at any time.

Does the tool connect to my exchange?

No. This tool never connects to any exchange, wallet, or brokerage account.

Does the tool place trades?

No. This is a record-keeping and analysis tool only — it never executes trades.

Does the tool ask for my private key?

No, and it never will. This tool never requests seed phrases, private keys, passwords, or exchange API secrets.

Is this tool financial advice?

No. This tool is designed for personal record-keeping, education, and historical performance analysis. It does not provide financial advice, trading signals, or guaranteed returns.
