What is the difference between sample standard deviation and population standard deviation?

Sample standard deviation divides by n-1 (using n data points), while population standard deviation divides by n; sample is used when data represents a subset of a larger population.

How does standard deviation measure data dispersion around the mean?

Standard deviation quantifies how spread out data points are from the mean; a larger value indicates greater variability in the dataset.

What data format does the calculator accept?

Enter numbers separated by commas, and the tool automatically parses them for calculation.

Which should I use, sample or population standard deviation?

Use sample if your data is a subset representing a larger population; use population only if your dataset is the complete population being studied.
