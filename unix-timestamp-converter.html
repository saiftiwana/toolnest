Tips for Best Results

What is a Unix epoch timestamp and how is it calculated?

A Unix timestamp counts the number of seconds elapsed since January 1, 1970 (UTC), commonly used to represent dates in computing systems.

How to convert Unix time to a human-readable date format?

Are Unix timestamps in seconds or milliseconds?

Traditional Unix timestamps use seconds, though some systems like JavaScript's Date.now() return milliseconds — check which your source uses.
