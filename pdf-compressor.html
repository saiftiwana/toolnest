How to Compress a PDF Online

  1. Select PDF: Upload your large PDF document into the browser compression tool.
  2. Optimize Structure: The tool strips unnecessary metadata and optimizes the internal file structure.

How It Works

This PDF size reducer and shrink-PDF-file tool optimizes the document's internal object structure directly in your browser — a free, privacy-first alternative to SodaPDF and Sejda's PDF compressor, since client-side compression keeps confidential files off any remote server.

How to compress large PDF documents safely on mobile?

The compressor runs in your mobile browser just like on desktop — upload the file and tap Compress to shrink it locally.

Why is client-side PDF compression safer for confidential files?

Because the file never leaves your device, there's no risk of a confidential document being intercepted or stored on a third-party server.
