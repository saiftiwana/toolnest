Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

ToolNest.link is a simple informational website: tool guides and comparisons. We do not run user accounts, comments, newsletters, or contact forms, and we do not ask you for your name, email address, or any other personal information. This page explains the limited data that is still involved when you visit, mostly because of affiliate links and web hosting.

Information we collect directly: none

There is nothing to sign up for and nowhere to submit personal information on this site. If you email us (see Contact below), we will only use your message to reply to you.

Cookies from affiliate links (Amazon)

Some buttons on our guides are affiliate links to Amazon. When you click one, Amazon may set cookies in your browser so it can tell that the visit came from ToolNest and pay us a commission if you buy something. The usual Amazon referral cookie lasts 24 hours; if you add a product to your cart during that visit, the attribution window for that cart can extend up to 90 days. These cookies are set and controlled by Amazon, not by ToolNest, and we cannot see who you are or what you personally bought — Amazon only reports aggregate commissions to us. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time; the guides will still work, but Amazon will no longer be able to attribute a purchase to ToolNest. Our full affiliate explanation is on the Affiliate Disclosure page.

Hosting and security data (Cloudflare)

This site is hosted on Cloudflare, which delivers the pages and protects the site from abuse. Like most hosts, Cloudflare may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser type, and pages requested, in server logs used for security and performance. We do not use that data to identify individual visitors, and we do not combine it with any other data about you. We do not run analytics tools that build profiles of our visitors.

How we use information

  • To operate and secure the website.
  • To understand, in aggregate, which guides readers find useful.
  • To receive affiliate commissions for qualifying purchases made through our links.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. Information is only handled by the services described above: Amazon (when you click an affiliate link) and Cloudflare (hosting and security), each under their own privacy policies.

Children's privacy

ToolNest is a general-audience site about tools and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be noted here.
