Editorial Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This page sets out the rules ToolNest publishes by. They apply to every guide on the site, and they do not change for any product, brand or season.

Independence

ToolNest is an independent site. It is not owned by, endorsed by, or affiliated with Amazon or any tool brand. Picks, rankings and verdicts are decided by ToolNest's own research process — described on the How We Choose page — and by nothing else.

Affiliate disclosure

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Some links on ToolNest — the "Check Price" and buy buttons on our guides — are Amazon affiliate links. If you click one and buy, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. The commission is the same whichever pick you choose, so it never influences which tool we recommend. The full disclosure is on the Affiliate Disclosure page.

No sponsored rankings

No brand can pay to be included in a guide, to be ranked higher, or to receive a better verdict. There are no sponsored placements inside our shortlists and no paid reviews. If a product is in a guide, it earned its place on specs, verified ratings and value — and its stated trade-off appears next to it either way.

Research standard — no invented experience

ToolNest guides are research-based. We do not claim hands-on testing, workshop hours or personal use that did not happen. Picks rest on manufacturer specifications for the exact model, verified Amazon ratings and review counts, and patterns in owner feedback. Where a detail cannot be verified, the guide says so rather than printing a confident guess.

Pricing policy

Amazon prices change too often for a printed number to stay true, so ToolNest does not display prices it cannot verify live. Guide buttons say "Check price" and lead to the live listing, where the current price is shown by Amazon. Any price reference that appears in a guide is clearly framed as approximate and time-stamped by the guide's date.

Product images

Product photos in our guides show the exact model being discussed, so readers can identify it confidently on the listing. Product names, images and trademarks belong to their respective owners, and their use on ToolNest is for identification only — never to imply endorsement.

Corrections policy

If we get something wrong — a spec, a rating, a model detail — we fix it openly. Corrections are made directly in the guide, and every guide carries its date so readers can judge how current it is. Readers can flag an error any time at toolnest.link@gmail.com; flagged items are re-checked against the manufacturer's specifications and the live listing before the guide is amended.

Updates to this policy

If this policy changes, the change will be published on this page with a new date at the top. Questions about it are welcome via the Contact page.
