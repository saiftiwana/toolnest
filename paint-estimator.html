Paint Estimator

Estimate total paint needed and rough cost for a house — free, instant.

How to Use the Paint Estimator

  1. Enter Room Metrics: Input total wall area and number of coats.
  2. Set Coverage Rate: Input paint coverage per liter and price per liter.
  3. Calculate Liters: View total paint quantity and estimated cost.

Why Use This Tool

Estimating how much wall paint you need in liters, and the total cost, online for free saves guesswork before a painting project. This tool calculates paint quantity from wall area, number of coats, and coverage rate, plus an optional total cost estimate. It prevents the common mistake of underestimating paint quantity and running short mid-project.

How It Works

This paint-coverage-calculator multiplies total wall area by the number of coats, then divides by your paint's stated coverage rate (square feet per liter) to determine total liters needed, optionally multiplying by price per liter for a cost estimate. It's a free browser alternative to Dulux and Benjamin Moore's paint calculators for quick project budgeting.

Key Features

The wall-area-paint-estimator accounts for multiple coats directly in its calculation, since most painting projects require at least two coats for even, opaque coverage. As a paint-liters-needed tool, entering an optional price per liter automatically calculates total project cost alongside the raw liter quantity. An adjustable wastage percentage produces a rounded-up recommended purchase quantity, and cost per square foot is broken out separately for easier comparison across paint brands.

Common Use Cases

Homeowners planning a room repaint calculate exactly how much paint to purchase before a hardware store trip. Painting contractors provide clients with a material cost estimate based on wall area. DIY renovators budget for a multi-room painting project by calculating each room separately, and property managers estimate paint costs across several rental units. Interior designers provide clients with a rough paint budget line item during a renovation planning phase.

Tips for Best Results

Always check your specific paint product's stated coverage rate on the label, since this varies significantly between paint types and brands — using a generic assumed coverage rate can lead to under- or over-purchasing. Remember to subtract door and window areas from your total wall measurement for a more accurate paint quantity estimate. Buying a slightly larger container than the exact calculated amount is often more cost-effective than purchasing multiple smaller cans.

A Quick Note

A little extra paint left over is far less frustrating than an unexpected mid-project trip back to the store for one more can.

Privacy & Security

Because ToolNest processes everything entirely within your browser using client-side JavaScript, your files never leave your device. There is no server-side storage, no third-party analytics attached to your data, and no risk of a breach exposing files you processed months ago.

How many square feet does 1 liter or gallon of wall paint typically cover?

Coverage varies by paint type and brand, commonly ranging from 100-150 square feet per liter; always check your specific product's label.

How do I calculate room wall area excluding doors and windows for painting?

Measure total wall area, then subtract the area of doors and windows to get the actual paintable surface area.

Does the calculator account for multiple coats of paint?

Yes, enter your planned number of coats and the calculation multiplies wall area accordingly.

Can I get a total cost estimate along with the paint quantity?

Yes, enter your price per liter and the tool calculates estimated total project cost automatically.
