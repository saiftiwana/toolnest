Wedding Planning Guide, Features & FAQ

How to Use This Tool

  1. Wedding Setup: Enter the bride, groom, date, country, and currency in Step 01.
  2. Add Events: Add whichever events apply — Mehndi, Nikah, Baraat, Walima, or a custom event — each gets its own budget and guest count.
  3. Plan Everything: Use the workspace tabs for Budget, Guests, Catering, Venue, Vendors & Expenses, Invitations, Checklist, and Timeline — fill in only what's relevant to your wedding.
  4. Review & Share: Check your Dashboard for a complete overview, generate your invitation, and print or save your full report.

Why Use This Tool

Planning a Shaadi usually means juggling a budget spreadsheet, a guest list in one app, vendor contacts in another, and a separate invitation maker — this Studio brings budget planning, guest and RSVP management, catering estimates, vendor and payment tracking, a checklist, a timeline, and a bilingual Urdu/English invitation generator into one workspace, built around Pakistani and Indian wedding events like Mehndi, Nikah, Baraat, and Walima specifically.

How It Works

Every event, guest, expense, and task you add is calculated with simple, transparent deterministic formulas — total budget versus planned cost, remaining balance, guest counts per event, catering cost per head. Nothing is looked up automatically and no price is ever assumed on your behalf; your invitation and WhatsApp text are generated entirely from what you type, right in your browser.

Tips for Best Results

Add your events first, since guest lists, catering, venue, and vendor entries all reference them. Set your contingency percentage early — most experienced planners recommend keeping 10-15% of your budget unallocated for last-minute costs. Use the Vendor & Expenses category dropdown consistently so your Dashboard's budget breakdown stays accurate. Generate your invitation only after your event details are finalized, since the date, time, and venue pull directly from what you've entered.

Wedding Planning Guide

How to make a wedding budget

Start with a realistic total budget, then break it down across categories like venue, catering, décor, and outfits — leaving a contingency percentage unallocated for unexpected costs is a common practice among experienced planners.

How to divide wedding expenses

Many families split costs by category or by side (bride's family vs groom's family) according to their own tradition and agreement — this tool lets you track costs by category and by event without assuming any particular division.

How to estimate catering costs

Multiply your confirmed guest count by your price-per-guest, with separate rates for adults and children if your caterer offers them — always confirm final headcount close to the event date since catering is usually one of the largest expenses.

How to organize a wedding guest list

Group guests by family where possible to speed up counting, tag each guest's side (bride, groom, or both), and assign them to specific events since not every guest attends every function.

How to track RSVP

Update each guest's RSVP status as responses come in — Pending, Invited, Confirmed, Declined, or Maybe — and use the filters to quickly see who's still unconfirmed close to your event date.

How to plan multiple wedding events

Give each event — Mehndi, Nikah, Baraat, Walima, or any other — its own budget, guest count, and venue, since expenses and headcounts often vary significantly between functions.

How to control wedding expenses

Track planned versus actual cost for every vendor and category, and check your Dashboard regularly for over-budget alerts before costs get out of hand.

How to track vendor payments

Record the quoted cost, final cost, and advance paid for every vendor as you book them, so your remaining balance is always visible before final payments come due.

How to prepare a wedding checklist

Work through tasks in three phases — before the wedding, event preparation, and final week — and add any custom tasks specific to your own family's traditions.

How to create a WhatsApp wedding invitation

Fill in your event and venue details in the Invitations tab, generate the WhatsApp text, and either copy it or tap Share on WhatsApp to send it yourself — nothing is sent automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I plan multiple wedding events like Mehndi, Baraat, and Walima separately?

Yes, add each event you're having in the Events tab, and every other module — budget, guests, catering, venue — can be linked to a specific event.

Can I create a wedding invitation in Urdu?

Yes, select Urdu or Bilingual as your invitation language — the Urdu output uses proper right-to-left layout.

Can I share my invitation on WhatsApp?

Yes, generate the WhatsApp text and either copy it or tap Share on WhatsApp — nothing is sent automatically, you're always in control.

How do I track guest RSVPs?

Update each guest's RSVP status as they respond, and use the Bride/Groom side and RSVP filters in the Guests tab to see who's confirmed, pending, or declined.

Can I calculate my catering cost based on guest count?

Yes, the Catering tab multiplies your adult and child guest counts by your own entered per-guest price for each event.

Can I track payments to multiple vendors?

Yes, the Vendors & Expenses tab covers every vendor category and tracks quoted cost, final cost, advance paid, and remaining balance for each.

Does this tool store my guest or family information?

No, everything is processed and kept in your browser. If you choose to save a draft, it's stored only in your browser's local storage, and you can clear it anytime.

Can I use this for a wedding in both Pakistan and India?

Yes, select your country in Step 01 to set the relevant currency and terminology — the tool works for weddings in either country.

Can I print a full wedding planning report?

Yes, the Report section generates a complete summary of your budget, guests, vendors, and checklist progress that you can print or save.

Does this tool provide fixed prices for vendors or venues?

No, all prices, rates, and vendor costs are entered by you — this tool never invents or predicts costs on your behalf.
