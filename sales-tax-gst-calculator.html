How to Use the Sales Tax / GST Calculator

  1. Enter Base Amount: Input the net price or total invoice sum.
  2. Set Tax Rate: Type your local Sales Tax, GST, or VAT percentage.
  3. Choose Mode: Click "Add Tax" or "Remove Tax" to see the breakdown.

Why Use This Calculator

Calculating gross and net sales tax or GST amounts, both inclusive and exclusive, online for free saves manual math for everyday pricing and invoicing tasks. This tool adds tax to a base amount or extracts the pre-tax price from a tax-inclusive total, both in one interface.

How It Works

This GST-calculator and sales-tax-remover applies straightforward percentage math in both directions — multiplying by the tax rate to add tax exclusive-style, or dividing by (1 + rate) to extract the pre-tax base from an inclusive total. It's a free, instant, local alternative to Calculator.net's sales tax calculator for quick everyday tax math.

Key Features

The gross-to-net-tax tool works in both common business scenarios: adding tax onto a known base price, or working backward from a total that already includes tax to find the original pre-tax amount. As a VAT-calculator, the tax rate field accepts any percentage, making it flexible for GST, VAT, or general sales tax rates across different regions. Results now clearly label net, tax, and gross amounts for both directions, an optional currency symbol displays throughout, and you can copy the full breakdown or reset the tool in one click.

Common Use Cases

Small business owners calculate the tax-inclusive price to charge a customer from a known base cost. Shoppers verify how much tax was included in a receipt total to understand the pre-tax price. Freelancers calculating an invoice add the correct tax percentage to their base service fee, and accountants double-check tax calculations on transaction records. Market vendors quickly add tax to a base price before quoting a final total to a customer.

Tips for Best Results

Double-check whether your local tax system quotes prices as tax-inclusive or tax-exclusive by default, since using the wrong mode (Add versus Remove) on a total will produce an incorrect result. Keep your tax rate percentage accurate and current, since rates occasionally change and an outdated rate will skew every calculation.

A Quick Note

Tax rates and rules vary by region and change periodically, so consult local guidance or an accountant for anything beyond a quick estimate.

How do you calculate tax-inclusive vs. tax-exclusive price amounts?

Tax-exclusive pricing adds tax on top of a base price, while tax-inclusive pricing means the tax is already built into the total, requiring division to extract the base amount.

How to quickly deduct a sales tax percentage from a final total bill?

Use the Remove Tax (Inclusive) mode, which divides the total by (1 + tax rate) to calculate the original pre-tax base amount.

Does the calculator work for GST, VAT, and general sales tax alike?

Yes, the tool works with any percentage-based tax rate regardless of what it's called in your region.

What happens if I use the wrong Add/Remove mode?

Using the wrong mode on an already-taxed total will produce an incorrect result; always confirm whether your starting amount includes tax already.
