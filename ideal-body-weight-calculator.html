Why Use This Calculator

Estimating your ideal body weight based on height and gender online for free gives a quick reference point using an established medical formula. This calculator applies the Devine formula, one of the most widely referenced methods, to estimate a target weight range in seconds.

How It Works

This IBW formula calculator uses the Devine formula — a standard baseline of 50kg for men and 45.5kg for women at 5 feet, adding 2.3kg for each additional inch of height — to estimate a target weight range, calculated instantly client-side. It's a free, instant local alternative to Omni Calculator's IBW tool for a quick reference estimate.

The target-weight-calculator adjusts its baseline calculation based on the gender selected, since the Devine formula uses different starting constants for men and women. Tips for Best Results

Remember this is a general population formula and doesn't account for individual factors like frame size, muscle mass, or body composition — treat the result as a general reference range rather than a precise personal target. Different formulas (Devine, Robinson, Miller, Hamwi) can produce slightly different estimates, so small variations between calculators are normal and expected.

What general factors influence ideal body weight calculations?

Height and gender are the primary inputs in most standard formulas, though frame size and body composition also affect what's healthy for an individual.

How do standard health formulas estimate target weight ranges?

Formulas like Devine's use a baseline weight for a reference height, then add a fixed amount per inch of additional height.

Why do different ideal weight formulas give different results?

Formulas like Devine, Robinson, Miller, and Hamwi use slightly different baseline constants, producing modestly different estimates for the same height.

Should I treat this calculation as a strict personal target?

No, treat it as a general reference range rather than a precise individual target, since it doesn't account for frame size or muscle mass.
