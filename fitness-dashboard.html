How It Works

This instant body metrics bmi calorie and macro tracker online runs the same trusted formulas used by the individual BMI, Ideal Body Weight, and Calorie/Macro tools — the standard BMI formula, the Devine ideal body weight formula, and the Mifflin-St Jeor BMR equation — now connected so BMR flows into TDEE, TDEE flows into your goal-adjusted calorie target, and that target flows into your chosen macro split. An added US Navy Method body fat estimator uses neck, waist, and hip measurements as an optional extra data point alongside BMI.

Key Features

As a free fitness calculator no signup health data private tool, every calculation happens locally in your browser tab, with nothing uploaded anywhere. Four diet-type macro presets — Balanced, Low-Carb, High-Protein, and Keto — instantly recalculate protein, carb, and fat grams and redraw a live macro pie chart, while a Localized Daily Meal & Protein Budget Planner lets you switch between a Global/Western or South Asian/Local food list, build a day of meals against your calorie and protein targets with live progress bars, or auto-generate a starting sample plan in one click.

Common Use Cases

Someone starting a weight loss plan uses the goal selector and diet-type presets to see calories, protein, carbs and fat targets update together instead of doing the math separately. Someone comparing muscle-gain nutrition strategies switches between High-Protein and Balanced splits to see how the same calorie target reshapes into different macro grams. Someone planning meals around local food uses the South Asian/Local cuisine list to build a daily plan from roti, daal, biryani and curry options sized to their own calorie and protein budget, and downloads the finished plan as a one-page PDF fitness report.

Tips

Body fat % and TDEE are both estimates — the visual gauge and progress bars are meant to guide day-to-day decisions, not replace a lab-grade body composition scan or advice from a doctor or dietitian, especially for anyone managing a medical condition. Because the meal planner uses fixed representative food items rather than an exhaustive nutrition database, treat the Auto-Generate plan as a starting point to adjust portions from, not an exact prescription.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is all my personal health data stored only in my browser with zero server uploads?

Yes, every calculation in the Fitness Dashboard runs entirely client-side in your browser; nothing is uploaded to or stored on a server.

How do BMI, ideal body weight, and macro targets interconnect for fitness planning?

Your weight and height drive BMI and ideal body weight, while your BMR, activity level, and goal drive your calorie target, which is then split into macros — changing any core input recalculates everything connected to it.

Can I track fitness metrics across multiple calculations without creating an account?

Yes, no account or sign-up is required at any point — just enter your profile and calculate.

What activity level should I select for accurate TDEE estimation?

Select the level that best matches your typical week: Sedentary for little or no exercise, up through Extra Active for a physical job combined with intense training.

Are calculations 100% private and compliant with health data standards?

All processing happens locally in your browser with no data transmitted anywhere, so no personal health data ever leaves your device.

How does the dashboard help with weight loss vs. muscle gain goals?

Selecting Lose Weight or Gain Weight adjusts your daily calorie target by roughly 500 calories from maintenance, and the diet-type presets let you match a macro split to that goal.

How is Body Fat % calculated in this tool?

Body Fat % uses the US Navy Method, based on neck, waist, height, and (for women) hip circumference measurements you enter.

Can I plan meals for South Asian or local food instead of Western food?

Yes, the meal planner includes a dedicated South Asian/Local cuisine list alongside the Global/Western list, covering items like roti, daal, biryani, and curries.
