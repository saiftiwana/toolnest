How It Works

This PDF joiner and combine-PDF-documents tool loads each file's pages directly in your browser using client-side PDF parsing — a free, private alternative to iLovePDF and Smallpdf merge for anyone who wants to bind PDF pages together without uploading sensitive files anywhere.

Are my sensitive PDF files safe when merging online?

Yes — merging happens entirely in your browser. Your PDF files are never uploaded to any server.
