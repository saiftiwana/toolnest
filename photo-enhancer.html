Real estate agents brighten dim interior photos before listing a property online. Online sellers fix washed-out or underexposed product photos taken on older phone cameras. Tips for Best Results

Increase brightness first if the photo looks dark overall, then raise contrast slightly to bring back detail in shadows and highlights. Saturation above 130% can start to look unnatural on skin tones, so adjust it gradually while watching the live preview. For scanned text documents, boosting contrast alone often improves legibility more than adjusting the other two sliders.
