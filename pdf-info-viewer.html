Common Use Cases

Legal and administrative staff verify a document's stated author and creation date before accepting it as evidence of authenticity. IT teams check PDF version compatibility before importing files into older enterprise systems. Researchers inspect metadata on downloaded academic PDFs to confirm publication details match the citation. Publishers double-check the producer software field to confirm which export tool generated a submitted manuscript PDF.

Tips for Best Results

Not every PDF includes complete metadata — some files are exported with fields like author or creation date deliberately blank, which the tool will report as 'Not specified' rather than an error. If you need to edit metadata rather than just view it, that requires a separate PDF editing tool. If you need to confirm a document has not been altered since a certain date, cross-check the creation and modification timestamps shown in the results.

Privacy & Security

Because ToolNest processes everything entirely within your browser using client-side JavaScript, your files never leave your device. There is no server-side storage, no third-party analytics attached to your data, and no risk of a breach exposing files you processed months ago.

What hidden information is stored inside a PDF document?
