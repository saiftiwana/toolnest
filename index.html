How we choose

We did not hands-on test these tools. Our picks are based on manufacturer specifications and verified owner reviews. That honesty is the point: we would rather show you real ratings and real specs than pretend we ran a lab we don't have.

  • Specs first: voltage, torque, battery, charging and what's in the box — from the manufacturer, not from roundup copy.
  • Verified ratings: star ratings and review counts from real listings, checked at time of writing.
  • Honest trade-offs: every pick gets the drawback that matters, not just the selling points.
  • Prices as ranges: tool prices move, especially around Black Friday — we show ranges, never a promised price.

Questions, answered
