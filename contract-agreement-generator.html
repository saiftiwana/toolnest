Why Use This Tool

A free contract generator online tool turns a guided set of questions into a structured business agreement draft, instead of starting from a blank page or reusing an old contract that doesn't quite fit the new situation. This simple contract agreement generator online tool covers six common agreement types, each with fields tailored to that specific kind of relationship, and clearly states throughout that it produces a draft for informational purposes rather than legal advice.

How It Works

This freelance contract generator online tool builds each agreement from a reusable clause structure — parties, term, scope, payment, revisions, intellectual property, confidentiality, termination, and dispute resolution — with fields and defaults adjusted for the agreement type you select. An NDA / Confidentiality Agreement uses a genuinely different structure focused on disclosing and receiving parties, confidential information, and duration, rather than reusing the general service-agreement layout. Every clause is generated only when relevant information is provided, so optional sections are cleanly omitted rather than left with empty placeholder text.

Key Features

As a service agreement generator tool, six agreement types are supported — Freelance Service Agreement, Website Development Agreement, Consulting Agreement, Independent Contractor Agreement, Vendor/Business Agreement, and NDA/Confidentiality Agreement — each generating a properly structured draft rather than one generic template stretched across every use case. Payment supports fixed fee, hourly, or milestone-based structures with a configurable schedule, revision terms can be unlimited, a fixed number, or none, and intellectual property ownership, confidentiality, termination notice, and dispute resolution method are all independently configurable. A repeatable custom clause builder covers anything not already included, and the finished draft exports as PDF, DOCX, plain text, or a print-ready page.

Common Use Cases

A freelance web designer starts a new project by generating a Website Development Agreement, listing specific deliverables like homepage design and a contact form, and setting milestone-based payments tied to project phases. A consultant sharing sensitive business information with a new client first sends an NDA generated from the dedicated NDA fields, clearly defining what counts as confidential and for how long. A small agency taking on a new vendor relationship uses the Vendor/Business Agreement type, adds a custom clause about delivery timelines specific to their industry, and exports a PDF ready for both parties to review.

Tips

Because this tool creates a draft rather than a finished legal document, review it against your specific situation and jurisdiction before use, and consult a qualified legal professional for anything with significant financial or legal stakes. The custom clause builder is useful for anything specific to your industry or relationship that the standard sections don't cover — a delivery timeline penalty, a specific software license, or an unusual payment condition can all be added as their own clause.
