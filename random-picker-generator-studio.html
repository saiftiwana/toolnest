How It Works

This online random number and name picker tool draws every result from your browser's cryptographically secure random source rather than a simple pseudo-random formula, using rejection sampling to keep every outcome genuinely evenly distributed across the range you choose — no subtle bias toward certain numbers or names. The Decision Wheel calculates an exact landing angle for a randomly chosen segment before it ever starts spinning, so the multi-second spin animation is just a satisfying reveal of an already-fair result, not a rigged one.

Key Features

As a random team generator and decision wheel tool, the Name/Team Picker handles three related jobs from one pasted list: picking one or more winners, splitting everyone evenly into teams, or simply shuffling the order. The Decision Wheel accepts up to 10 custom options with a real spinning animation and a fixed pointer, dice rolling supports up to ten dice from d4 to d20 at once, and the password generator draws from a true cryptographic random source with adjustable length, character types, and an option to exclude visually ambiguous characters like 0/O and 1/l/I.

Tips

For raffles or giveaways, paste every entry once (duplicates count as extra chances, so repeat a name if they should have better odds) and use Pick Winner(s) rather than manually scrolling through a list. For passwords meant to be remembered rather than saved in a manager, a longer length with fewer symbol characters is often easier to type accurately than a short one packed with symbols — the strength indicator updates either way so you can see the real trade-off.

Frequently Asked Questions

How random is the random number generator?

It uses your browser's cryptographically secure random number source with rejection sampling to avoid the subtle bias that simple random formulas can introduce, so every number in your chosen range has an equal chance of appearing.

Can I use the name picker for a classroom or raffle?

Yes, paste one name or entry per line and choose how many winners to pick — useful for calling on students, drawing raffle winners, or picking a volunteer fairly.

How does the decision wheel work?

Add your own options, and the wheel calculates a fair random result before spinning, then animates to land exactly on that outcome.

Can I split a list into random teams?

Yes, choose "Split into teams," set how many teams you need, and the list is shuffled and divided as evenly as possible.

Is the password generator actually secure?

Yes, it uses your browser's built-in cryptographic random number generator (the same type of secure randomness used for encryption keys), not a simple non-secure random function.

How many dice can I roll at once?

Up to 10 dice per roll, with your choice of d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, or d20.

Can I exclude confusing characters from a generated password?

Yes, enable "Exclude ambiguous characters" to remove characters that look similar, like 0/O and 1/l/I, which is useful for passwords you'll need to type from memory or read aloud.

Does this tool store or see my names, numbers, or passwords?

No, everything runs locally in your browser — nothing you enter or generate is sent to or stored on a server.
